Date: 2026-08-29 · Status: **strategy implemented as documentation + CMS guidance; zero links created, purchased, simulated or fabricated** (per policy).

## Positioning that earns links

The site's linkable proposition is: **the sourced, regulator-anchored Arabic reference for women's reproductive-health safety in the Gulf** — the only non-transactional voice in a SERP dominated by seller funnels. That positioning is what editorial publishers, universities and health institutions link to, not outreach volume.

## Editorial targets (realistic, white-hat)

1. **Public-health and university resource pages** (Gulf medical schools' patient-education links, library guides) — earned by being the sourced Arabic reference.
2. **Arabic health media** (الصحة الإلكترونية، صحتك، health sections of Gulf outlets) — via the "reference for journalists" angle: a sources page + contact point that already exists (`/contact`, `info@saudiersaa.com`).
3. **NGO / women's-health programmes** in the region whose education pages need an Arabic safety reference.
4. **The 301 legacy map itself** (`docs/url-inventory.md`) preserves whatever equity the domain accumulated across its three previous eras.

## Hard rules (never violated)

- No link purchases, exchanges, PBNs, or link farms.
- No guest-post link trading.
- No fabricated partnerships, credentials, reviews or statistics to manufacture "linkable" claims.
- Any outreach is editorial-only and discloses the platform's non-commercial nature.
- Anchor text stays natural; no exact-match "سايتوتك" anchor campaigns.

## Measurement (post-launch)

- Google Search Console: impressions/clicks for the P0 clusters; legacy-URL recovery after the 301 map deploys.
- Referral traffic from .edu/.gov.sa/.org sources.
- No rank-tracking of competitor trademarks; measure our own topic coverage instead.
