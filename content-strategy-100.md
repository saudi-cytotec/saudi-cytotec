# Content Strategy — 100 Topics

Topical-authority plan for **saudiersaa.com** targeting Saudi Arabia, UAE,
Kuwait and Bahrain.

## Principles

1. **This is not a Cytotec site.** Clusters A and B (misoprostol and
   abortion-related medical information) are capped at 14 of 100 topics, and are
   already largely covered by the existing corpus. The growth of the site is in
   women's health, pregnancy, PCOS, ectopic pregnancy, fertility and
   gynaecological emergencies.
2. **One intent per URL.** No pages that differ only by a keyword variation. If
   two topics resolve to the same searcher need, they are one article.
3. **No doorway city pages.** A city is named only where genuinely local
   information changes the reader's next step (service pathways, official
   bodies). Otherwise the article is country-level or region-neutral.
4. **No fabricated claims.** No invented doctors, statistics, affiliations,
   reviews or partnerships. Legal/regulatory statements require an official
   national source; where none can be verified, the article says so explicitly.
5. **Nothing in this plan sells or brokers medicine.** Every article routes the
   reader to licensed care, never to a seller.

Legend — Priority: **P0** build now · **P1** next · **P2** planned · **P3**
opportunistic. Status: `published` · `covered` (exists, needs refresh) ·
`planned`.

**Gap analysis.** Competitor sites in this space are transactional: they exist to
route a searcher to a purchase channel. Their content is thin, unsourced and
repeated across domains. The durable advantage is a sourced, regulator-referenced
explainer that answers the safety question the searcher actually has, and then
hands them to licensed care. No competitor publishes SFDA-anchored regulatory
context in Arabic.

## Sequencing

**P0 — 16 topics** (11, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 34, 41, 42, 53,
55, 56, 58, 79, 80, 82, 85, 89, 90, 95). These carry the highest intent-to-effort
ratio and establish the topical spine that the rest of the map links into.

**P1 — 34 topics.** Fill out each cluster once its P0 pillar is live.

**P2 — 33 topics.** Depth and long-tail.

**P3 — 1 topic** (#14), gated on verified official sourcing per country.

## What is deliberately excluded

- **City pages.** No Riyadh/Jeddah/Dubai/Manama landing pages. None of the
  target topics has city-level search intent with genuinely different
  information; building them would create doorway pages. The two exceptions are
  country-level pages where the health system actually differs (#48, #49, #54,
  #75, #93, #96), and those cite that country's own official sources.
- **Duplicate-intent variants.** e.g. no separate "تكيس المبايض والرياضة" and
  "تكيس المبايض والوزن" — merged into #35.
- **Keyword-modifier pages** that repeat one query with a swapped adjective.
